Commissions are the second half of how Plexi gets funded. Sponsorship covers baseline runway. Commissions cover the upside, and they double as the content engine that drives sponsorship in the first place. One transaction, three deliverables.

**$500 flat. Here's what you get:**

- A custom Plexi app, built to a brief you write. It runs on your machine, lives in your apps directory, and is yours. MIT-licensed, source code on GitHub.
- A 10-to-18 minute tentpole YouTube video where I build it on camera, with your name in the title card and the description. You're the protagonist of the video. The app is the artifact.
- A line in the public commissions log on this site, with a link to the video and the source.

**What kinds of apps make sense at $500:**

A Spotify controller pane that surfaces what's playing and binds play/pause/skip to global hotkeys. A focus timer with custom intervals and a small heads-up display. A personal dashboard that pulls from two or three APIs you already use — GitHub, Linear, your weather station — and lays it out the way your brain actually wants to see it. A tail of your build server's log with structured highlighting. A scratch pane for whatever single workflow eats five minutes of your day.

These all fit because they're one process, one purpose, one screen. They live inside Plexi. They use my SDK and my capability broker. The app is small enough to ship in a single session and tight enough to make a good video.

**What doesn't fit:**

Anything that needs a server I have to run for you. Anything that requires ongoing support, account management, or a database I have to maintain. Anything that depends on a platform I don't have access to (locked-down enterprise SaaS, undocumented protocols, scraping sites that actively fight back). Anything where the actual value is in the brief itself — i.e. you want me to design the product, not build it. The brief is your job. The build is mine.

If the idea fits, the form's open. If it doesn't, I'll tell you fast and you'll get the money back before the email chain hits four messages.
