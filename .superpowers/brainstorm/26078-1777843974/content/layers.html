Ribbon #563 — complementary, not conflicting
Ribbon is the navigation model for Z1 (horizontal priority queue). Overlays float above it. They don't interact — the ribbon sorts the background; Z2/Z3 float on top of whatever position you're at. Ribbon left = priority, ribbon right = new arrivals, same as before.
Job pile #528 — already well-specced
