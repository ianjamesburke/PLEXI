### Proposed sprint

1. Truth first: `0701`.
2. Stable boundary: `0702`, `0703`, and existing DAW gate `0591`.
3. Product closeouts in parallel: plugin-state chain, pane-liveness chain, `0441`, consent chain, then WASM and frame-pacing closeouts.
4. Acceptance: zero-skip CI, `0590`/`0602` installed-stable sweep, then `0704` isolated release round trip.

Stop after the acceptance artifacts. Do not pull Assistant, orchestration, browser, marketplace, native WASM distribution, or media editing into this sprint.
